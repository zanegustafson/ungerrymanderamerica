About the Show

Every episode is recorded in person. Every conversation is in good faith.

The Show

Ungerrymander America is a podcast about the intertwined systems we all live within - politics, culture, economics, technology, food, education, and on and on. Through open and good faith conversation, together we can build a better system and create better conditions for human flourishing today, and far into the future.

Each episode tackles a different system because this show is about more than rebuilding our broken political system. The goal is to build a better world and to create better conditions for human flourishing today, and far into the future.

The Host

Ungerrymander America is hosted by Zane Gustafson. He is the author of Polemic for Democracy, a book about constitutional reform. He is based in Seattle and has expertise in the energy transition and democracy reform.

Every episode is recorded in person. It is a deliberate choice that brings a different energy and depth to each conversation.

The Name

Gerrymandering is the manipulation of political district boundaries to entrench power. "Ungerrymandering America" is about undoing that, and every other system that's been rigged against ordinary people. The show believes the map of a fair and representative America is possible. It's just going to take all of us to get there.

The Book

By Zane Gustafson

Polemic for Democracy

A book about constitutional reform

The ideas behind Ungerrymander America go deeper than a podcast. Polemic for Democracy is Zane Gustafson's case for rethinking the systems of American democracy from the ground up: the constitutional foundations, the structural problems, and what a genuinely representative America could look like. Reading it is one of the best ways to go in depth on the show's themes and to support Zane's work directly.
