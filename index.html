About the Show

Full about page →

A show about the intertwined systems we all live within

Politics, culture, economics, technology, food, education: each episode of Ungerrymander America explores a different system. Through open and good faith conversation, together we can build a better world and create better conditions for human flourishing today, and far into the future.

Hosted by Zane Gustafson, author of Polemic for Democracy, a book about constitutional reform. Based in Seattle, with expertise in the energy transition and democracy reform. Every episode is recorded in person.
