About the Show

Full about page →

A show about the intertwined systems we all live within

Politics, culture, economics, technology, food, education: each episode of Ungerrymander America explores a different system. Through good faith conversation and detailed analysis, the goal of the show is to build a better world and create better conditions for everyone today, and far into the future.

The show is hosted by Zane Gustafson. He is a graduate of the University of Washington and lives in Seattle. His professional expertise is in research for the energy transition and democracy reform.
