# State-Level Data Center Legislation Report
## Focus: 2025-2026

---

## Summary

As of August 2026, data center regulation has become one of the most active areas of state-level policy in the country. New York became the first state to impose a statewide moratorium on large data centers. Multiple states have repealed or paused tax incentives. Virginia enacted the nation's first electricity consumption tax on data centers. Texas paused all pending approvals for a statewide audit. At least 11 states have attempted bans or moratoriums.

## Key Trends (2025-2026)

1. **Tax incentive rollback is accelerating.** Ohio's $1.6B surprise triggered a wave. Arizona, Illinois, North Carolina, and Oregon have all paused or repealed incentives. Virginia narrowly preserved its exemption but added a consumption tax.

2. **Moratoriums are proliferating.** New York is the first state-level moratorium. Pennsylvania, Georgia, Oregon, and Tennessee have local or proposed moratoriums. Ohio attempted a constitutional ban.

3. **Ratepayer protection is bipartisan.** Both Republican (Abbott in Texas) and Democratic (Hochul in New York, Kotek in Oregon) governors are acting to shield residential electricity customers from data center infrastructure costs.

4. **Water reporting is becoming standard.** Utah, Virginia, New York, New Jersey, Idaho, and Pennsylvania all introduced or enacted water use disclosure requirements.

5. **38 states still offer data center tax incentives**, but the political calculus has shifted dramatically in 2026.
